Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 9, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Denali Custom Homes LLC ("we," "us," or "our") collects, uses, and protects information you provide when you request a free estimate or interact with our SMS messaging service.

1. Information We Collect

We collect the following information when you submit a service request or contact us:

  • Name – your first and/or last name as provided
  • Phone number – used solely to communicate with you about your estimate request
  • Project details – description of the service or project you requested
  • Address – the service location you provide during scheduling

2. How We Use Your Information

We use your information exclusively to:

  • Respond to your estimate or service request
  • Schedule and confirm FREE in-home estimate appointments
  • Send appointment reminders via SMS
  • Follow up on your project inquiry

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information with third parties for marketing purposes.

3. SMS Messaging

By submitting a service request (via Thumbtack or by contacting us directly), you consent to receive SMS text messages from Denali Custom Homes LLC related to your request. These messages may include:

  • Appointment scheduling and confirmation messages
  • Appointment reminders
  • Follow-up communications about your project

Message frequency: Varies based on your interaction with us, typically 2–5 messages per estimate request.

Message and data rates may apply depending on your mobile carrier plan.

To opt out: Reply STOP to any message at any time. You will receive a final confirmation and no further messages will be sent. Reply HELP for assistance.

4. Data Retention

We retain your contact information for the duration of our business relationship and for up to 12 months thereafter for record-keeping purposes, unless you request deletion.

5. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect your information against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction. Your data is stored securely and access is limited to authorized personnel only.

6. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Request access to the personal information we hold about you
  • Request correction or deletion of your information
  • Opt out of SMS communications at any time by replying STOP
